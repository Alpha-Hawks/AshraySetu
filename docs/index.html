System Architecture

The 8 Core Resilient Modules (M1 – M8)

Architected for complete offline autonomy during severe cyclonic storms with zero external dependency on cloud or telecom infrastructure.

MODULE 1

Role & Auth Gateway

Frictionless anonymous session generation for field volunteers and role-based access for District Emergency Operation Centres.

MODULE 2

Evacuee Intake & Triage Engine

High-contrast bilingual intake form. Prioritizes pregnant women, bedridden patients, infants, and livestock.

MODULE 3

QR Token & Verification Subsystem

Encodes family demographics into an ultra-compact visual token pass rendered without internet; verified in 0.0 seconds.

MODULE 4

Shelter Inventory & Resource Ledger

Continuous accounting of potable water and rations based on Sphere standards, raising replenishment alarms under 24 hours.

MODULE 5

Offline Storage & Sync Engine

Browser-native IndexedDB via Dexie.js. Auto-synchronizes with vector conflict resolution when network restores.

MODULE 6

Spatial GIS & Proximity Subsystem

Interactive Leaflet map with geodesic routing to nearest shelters and Turf.js 3.5m - 5.0m storm surge coastal inundation buffers.

MODULE 7

District Command Desk

Administrative console for BDOs and District Collectors, visualizing capacity heatmaps and medical priority queues.

MODULE 8

Emergency Dispatch Module

Automated Telegram and SMS emergency dispatch payloads formatted with coordinates to mobilize water tankers and NDRF platoons.
